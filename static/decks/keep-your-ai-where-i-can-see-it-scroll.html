Appendix C: Browser Chat vs Persistent Workspace

Browser chat Persistent workspace
Produces an answer Produces durable work
Context is supplied temporarily Context persists in project files
Memory is largely service-controlled Project memory can live in team-controlled files
Suggests what someone might do Reads, writes, uses tools, and checks its work
Share a transcript Share files, diffs, and review
Improve the prompt Improve the knowledge bundle, instructions, and workflow
Conversation history Version history

Appendix D: Peter's Quick Takes (Let's Discuss)

  • USC Accounts
    • ChatGPT -> Codex.
      • Faculty and students can request access from USC: https://itservices.usc.edu/2026/02/26/new-features-now-available-in-uscs-chatgpt-edu-workspace/
      • I'm yet to test how USC account's capacity compares to paid plans; as provided, the units of measurement are apples and oranges.
    • Google -> Gemini. Pro (paid) account provides a little bit more than USC accounts in terms of models, but not tremendously more or better.
  • Gemini (free) - the free account can do CLI/workspaces (the focus of this entire slideshow) while Claude and Codex cannot. This makes it great for workshops or any other situation when I don't want to push students to pay. It has less capacity than paid and USC accounts, naturally.
  • LLMs Have compute levels - "effort". Productively toggling among them is a learned skill.
            
  • U.S. Frontier Models
    • Claude and Codex are peers. Gemini might catch up.
    • Their "CLIs" are Claude Code, (ChatGPT's) Codex, and (Gemini/Google's) Antigravity.
    • The default CLI is the terminal. For the Chinese models I've used, the terminal is the only choice.
    • Peter pays for - Claude, Codex, Gemini, Perplexity, DeepSeek (per token).
    • OpenAI just announced pay as you go - cheaper for their modest models. But only for the API, possibly not useful.
    • Get to know Claude's "Plan Mode".
    • Google: Google search, AI Mode, and Gemini are not the same thing.
    • When I hit my "Usage limit reached" in Claude Code, and then opt to pay for "Usage credits", that rate seems costly - $5 runs out quickly. Perhaps my context window is already costly at that point, or usage credits are a bad deal?
    • Meta has new models out that are worth watching.
  • Chinese LLMs. Our Chinese students use these.
    • DeepSeek, Qwen, Kimi, etc. compete with U.S. models and are cheap.
    • Often can be downloaded for privacy and control. So can Meta's recent release.
  • Perplexity
    • Handy for researching AI itself as it's functionally unbiased
    • Broader search too: $20/month for best alternative - ever - to Google search
  • Popular - Obsidian, VS Code, GitHub
  • Tokens and Context - learn how to conserve is a skill

  • Ask your LLM to respond with an HTML page - easier to read helps Guiding Question, above

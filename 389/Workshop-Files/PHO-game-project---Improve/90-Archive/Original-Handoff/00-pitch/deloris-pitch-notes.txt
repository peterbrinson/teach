LAST BOWL (Deloris response, 9/8, sent in chat not meeting)

The game is not about becoming efficient at somebody else's grief.

One room, one order, one person we slowly understand. The bowl is a ritual. The player should notice which seat has been repaired, the unopened mail, the raincoat still wet, the menu with one item circled. They make the bowl because care means paying attention.

No star rating. No waste score. No sprinting between stations. If ingredients can be “wrong,” the game should show what the choice means rather than throw a red X.

I want the player character visible if possible. Hands and posture make the act of preparing food feel specific. An overhead camera also lets the restaurant compose like a little stage.
